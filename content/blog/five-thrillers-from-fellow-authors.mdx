Some thrillers start with a badge that may already be compromised. Some start with a crash that everyone wants to call an accident. This post gathers five books from fellow authors I am sharing with readers: two at a little more length, then three shorter mentions, plus a free short story and my Ryan Kane prequel.

Covers for this same lineup are on the [San Antonio thriller picks page](/free-thriller/texas/san-antonio/). None of the five novels takes place in San Antonio. The Ryan Kane Series is not a Texas story either.

## Why these five

What holds the list together is a simple pressure. Someone is being told to accept a version of events that does not hold. In each of these books, the official story starts to slip.

## BROKEN CITY by Damon Harper

![Cover of BROKEN CITY by Damon Harper](/covers/broken-city.jpg)

**BROKEN CITY** (Damon Harper · A Corrupt Authority Psychological Thriller)

*Some cities die from neglect. Others are murdered on purpose.*

Some cities die from neglect. Others are murdered on purpose. Detective Malloy has spent her career holding East Baltimore together with sheer will. Now the deaths are coming faster, overdoses, stabbings, and disappearances with too many questions and not enough answers.

[Get BROKEN CITY on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G1X5MYV9)

The first line of Damon Harper's own description does the work: some cities die from neglect, others are murdered on purpose. Detective Malloy is holding East Baltimore together, with Officers Nia Wallace and Ryan Mitchell beside her, a missing grandson across the block, and a trail of falsified reports. It is one of the two longer looks in this set.

## Stalker by Tom Claren

![Cover of Stalker by Tom Claren](/covers/stalker.jpg)

**Stalker** (Tom Claren · A Psychological Thriller)

*Someone tries to kill my best friend. I vow to track them down.*

Someone tries to kill my best friend. I vow to track them down. When a truck rams our car on a dark country road, everyone calls it a terrible accident. I know better, because my best friend Mia has been hiding the fact that she has a stalker. Then the stalker turns his attention to me.

[Get Stalker free on BookFunnel](https://dl.bookfunnel.com/dt9msoegf9)

Tom Claren's Stalker is told in the first person, from a medical student who trusts evidence over excuses. After a truck rams the car she is in, everyone calls it a terrible accident. She knows better, because her best friend Mia has been hiding a stalker. Then the stalker turns his attention to her. It is the other longer look in this set, and it is free on BookFunnel.
