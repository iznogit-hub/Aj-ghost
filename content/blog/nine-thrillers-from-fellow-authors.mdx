A notepad that slides when its owner leaves the room. A voice in your head that does not belong to you. A murder posed like a shot list. A hidden seam in a penthouse wall. Music behind a door sealed in 1873. This week's letter puts nine fellow-author books in front of you: four with a longer look, five in brief, then a short story you can finish here and my prequel, HUNTED.

The visual cards for every title live on the [New Braunfels thriller picks page](/free-thriller/texas/new-braunfels/). Not one of the nine is set in New Braunfels. Ryan Kane's books are not set in Texas.

## What holds this list together

Each story begins at the second a person catches a detail nobody wanted noticed. An official version of events is already underway. You arrive just as that version starts to fail.

## Four features from fellow authors

### Skye Arden: the building that records what she misses

![Cover of She Was Never Alone by Skye Arden](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/81KX4h-GsBL._SL1500_.jpg)

**She Was Never Alone** (Skye Arden · A Psychological Thriller)

*In a building where every wall carries sound, every blind spot has an owner.*

Nina Calloway has spent eighteen years teaching people to trust their instincts. Then those instincts start to feel like symptoms. Her notepad moves while she is gone, then moves back. I am giving this title a longer look again because it is on this week's list, even if some readers already met it in an earlier letter. What I want to hold onto here is the building itself: a place that seems designed to keep you safe, and a record that will not stay where she left it.

[Get She Was Never Alone on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/She-Was-Never-Alone-psychological-ebook/dp/B0HP5BB2Q5)

### Christie Winter: a voice that is not his

![Cover of Silent Night Protocol by Christie Winter](https://bookfunnelimages.com/images/covers/zjn2rlaik5/cover_lg.jpg)

**Silent Night Protocol** (Christie Winter · A Psychological Thriller)

*What if the most dangerous voice in your head isn't yours?*

Winter's lead is named Ryan, and he is not Ryan Kane. He was recruited because he never breaks. Then the classified Silent Night Protocol starts to cost him: lost time, notes he does not remember writing, surveillance footage of himself carrying out missions he never chose. Commands appear without transmitters. Triggers fire with no source. Someone is controlling him. Or something is.

### Janet Walker: a murder left as an edit

![Cover of The Director's Cut by Janet Walker](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/81CboyITZ1L._SL1500_.jpg)

**The Director's Cut** (Janet Walker · A Nick Waters Detective Series)

*The killer isn't leaving clues. He's leaving edits.*

When a powerful entertainment lawyer is found dead, posed like a scene from a movie, detective Nick Waters recognizes a signature he saw five years ago, before the evidence was erased and he was forced to bury the case. Now the victim is connected to Zoe Valentine, Hollywood's untouchable golden girl, and reporter Maisie Kline realizes the story she once helped hide may be the reason people are dying.

[Get The Director's Cut on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HCM5WDSY)

### Parker Vale: a smile Claire does not trust

![Cover of The Deadly Partner by Parker Vale](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/71pHF7JM-5L._SL1500_.jpg)

**The Deadly Partner** (Parker Vale · A Claire Beckett Psychological Thriller)

*She knows a charming smile can hide a monster.*

Ben Carter is the man police questioned seven years ago when his girlfriend was found murdered. Another woman later accused him in public, and within days, she was dead too. Neither case was ever solved. Now he is with Lucy, and her friend Claire, who has been hunted before, is afraid Lucy won't survive it. With a blizzard closing in, Claire needs answers. A killer is still out there.

## Where HUNTED fits
