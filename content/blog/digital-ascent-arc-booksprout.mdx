Digital Ascent is book three of The Ryan Kane Series, and the advance copy is open. I am not going to walk you through the plot. A book that earns its dread does not survive a summary, and I will not spend your trust on one. What I will say is simpler. If you have been waiting to read ahead, the file is on BookSprout. If you have never met Ryan Kane, do not start at the end.

An advance reader copy is an early file, not a favor you have to repay in public. You request it. You read it when your nights allow. You stop if it is not yours. Nothing in that arrangement is a contract for stars, a quota of words, or a performance of enthusiasm. I would rather have one precise sentence from a reader who finished angry than a pile of praise from people who were hurried. If you review, tell the truth, including the parts that failed you. If you do not review, the copy was still yours to read.

That is the whole of the ask. Read at your own pace. An honest review is welcome. It is never required. Say so if you post one, so a stranger can tell an early file from the finished book. Do not invent a reaction you did not have. Do not pass the file around as if it were already on a shelf.

The series has an order, and the order matters more than the novelty of being early. HUNTED is the prequel, and it is free on BookFunnel. Fractured Ground is the novel that follows, on Kindle. Digital Ascent comes after those. Jumping to book three because the link is new is a good way to meet a man you do not know yet and blame the book for your confusion. I would rather you arrive late and oriented than early and lost.

If you want a few pages before you decide the prequel is worth a download, there is a short story on the site. [Pump Six](/free-reads/pump-six/) is companion fiction from the same world. It is not a chapter of any novel, and it is not set in the city whose page embeds it. The [Houston night-shift page](/free-thriller/texas/houston/) is for readers who want that story in a quieter frame, then the prequel afterward. The books themselves are not a Texas story. The page is only a door.

Take the advance copy if book three is where you already are. Take HUNTED if you are still outside. Either way, leave yourself enough night to finish what you start.
