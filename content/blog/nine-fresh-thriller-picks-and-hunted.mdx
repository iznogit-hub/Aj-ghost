A moved notepad. A voice that does not belong to its host. A killing arranged like a shot on a storyboard. A hairline gap in a penthouse wall. Music behind a door that has been shut since 1873. Those are the nine titles on this week's list: four given more space, five kept brief, then a short story you can finish in one sitting and my prequel, HUNTED.

The cover cards for the same set live on the [Longview thriller page](/free-thriller/texas/longview/). I am not saying any of these novels is set in Longview. Ryan Kane's story is not a Texas story either.

## What the nine have in common

Each book starts when a person clocks a detail that was supposed to stay hidden. A tidy official account is already in motion. You arrive at the moment that account begins to come apart.

## Four longer looks

### Skye Arden: a record that will not stay put

![Cover of She Was Never Alone by Skye Arden](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/81KX4h-GsBL._SL1500_.jpg)

**She Was Never Alone** · psychological thriller · on Kindle

*The office that should have been her safest room no longer feels like one.*

Nina Calloway has taught people, for eighteen years, to listen to the gut feeling that keeps them alive. Now that same gut feeling starts to read like an illness. A client who just moved into 5C insists that someone above her is keeping watch. Nina's notepad shifts while she is out, then returns to where it was. The building itself seems built to carry sound, and each unwatched corner already appears to belong to someone.

[Get She Was Never Alone on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/She-Was-Never-Alone-psychological-ebook/dp/B0HP5BB2Q5)

This title is on the list again this week, so I am giving it space. Some readers will already know it from an earlier letter. What I want to sit with here is the building: a place sold as shelter, and a record that will not stay put.

### Christie Winter: a voice that may not be his

![Cover of Silent Night Protocol by Christie Winter](https://bookfunnelimages.com/images/covers/zjn2rlaik5/cover_lg.jpg)

**Silent Night Protocol** · psychological thriller · free on BookFunnel with a newsletter sign-up

*The most dangerous voice he hears may not be his own.*

Winter's lead is also named Ryan, and he is not Ryan Kane. Recruiters picked him because he does not crack. Then a classified program called Silent Night Protocol starts taking pieces of him: stretches of time he cannot account for, notes in his handwriting that he cannot recall, and camera records of jobs he never agreed to run. Orders arrive with no transmitter behind them. Switches trip with no visible source. A person may be steering him. Or something else may be.

### Janet Walker: a murder staged as an edit

![Cover of The Director's Cut by Janet Walker](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/81CboyITZ1L._SL1500_.jpg)

**The Director's Cut** · a Nick Waters detective novel · on Kindle

*This killer does not plant clues. He plants cuts.*

A high-powered entertainment lawyer turns up dead, arranged as if a camera had blocked the scene. Detective Nick Waters knows the staging. He saw that same signature five years earlier, before the proof was wiped and he was made to let the file go. The new victim ties back to Zoe Valentine, Hollywood's unreachable star, and reporter Maisie Kline starts to fear that a story she once helped bury is now getting people killed.

[Get The Director's Cut on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HCM5WDSY)

### Parker Vale: a smile Claire will not trust

![Cover of The Deadly Partner by Parker Vale](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/71pHF7JM-5L._SL1500_.jpg)

**The Deadly Partner** · a Claire Beckett psychological thriller · pre-order on Kindle, out November 27

*Claire already knows how easily a warm smile can hide a predator.*

Seven years back, police questioned Ben Carter after his girlfriend was found murdered. Later, a second woman named him in public; within days she was dead as well. Both cases went cold. Ben is now with Lucy, and Claire, Lucy's friend, has been hunted before. She does not believe Lucy will live through this. A blizzard is closing the roads, and Claire has to find answers while a killer remains at large.

## The shorter five

### Evelyn Vale: a charge that will not hold

![Cover of One Cadet's Remorse by Evelyn Vale](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/719I4Jz9jcL._SL1500_.jpg)

**One Cadet's Remorse** · a One Lie Too Many psychological thriller · on Kindle

*A false charge and a closed institution stand in an ordinary woman's way.*

Olivia Gray goes to see her brother and walks into a crisis. Eric teaches at the Air Force Academy and is well regarded there, then faces a claim of misconduct involving a cadet. Olivia is sure the charge is false. The Academy's sealed culture stops her at every turn when she tries to clear him. Then the unsigned threats begin.

[Get One Cadet's Remorse on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DZY7BCFJ)

A West Wing concert hall has been locked since 1873, after a fire ended the lives of twelve gifted young players. Clara Burton, four months into the job of Assistant Headmistress, can hear that symphony at midnight. I am listing it as a Victorian supernatural mystery, the fourth entry in the Liminal Academy Chronicles, not as a thriller.

## Where HUNTED belongs on this list

All nine of those books turn on a fact somebody was not supposed to notice. My series meets that same pressure from Ryan Kane's side of a vanished night. HUNTED is the prequel. Ryan Kane sleeps in a car in Phoenix. After he dozes off beneath a tree, he comes to beside the vehicle. Seven hours are missing. His phone placed calls he cannot explain, and a blank white card he cannot account for is with him. These lines are taken straight from the manuscript:

> I checked my phone. 1:47 AM. (HUNTED · Chapter 3)

> Static. Then my voice. Slurred. Wrong. (HUNTED · Chapter 3)

> I'd gone to sleep at seven. (HUNTED · Chapter 3)

> The card didn't go into the storm drain. (HUNTED · Chapter 3)
