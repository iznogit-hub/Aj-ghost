Some psychological thrillers build their tension inside a single closed room. Others build it around an entire town that has already decided who is to blame before the facts are even on the table. Stump Lewis's Hollow Trust starts at a high-society gala in Wraith's Hollow, where a prominent figure collapses and every gaze swings toward the outsider in the crowd.

## When a town closes ranks around a secret

What makes a small-town cover-up work on the page is social gravity. Nobody needs to lock a literal gate when reputation, old money, and decades of unspoken agreements already push the same direction. When an outsider shows up in a place like Wraith's Hollow, the easiest story for the town to tell is that the newcomer brought the trouble with them.

In Hollow Trust, that pressure hits Dr. Mara Ellis immediately. She is a therapist, an outsider, and the last person seen with the victim before the collapse. Once the room settles on a convenient suspect, every glance and polite question starts pointing the same way. Fighting back means digging through decades of lies, betrayal, and buried trauma that powerful people would rather leave untouched.

## Where the Ryan Kane Series meets the same distrust

That same collision between what a person knows and what everyone around them insists is true runs straight through my own Ryan Kane books, though from a completely different angle. Instead of a town closing ranks from the outside, Ryan Kane has to contend with a mind that keeps dropping pieces of the night before.

In HUNTED, the Ryan Kane Series Prequel, Phoenix has been Ryan's parking spot for three weeks. He sleeps in the car. Memory fails him, his hands will not stay still, and a few details return anyway: a black sedan parked just far enough away, a phone pointed in his direction, a stranger writing something down. When nobody around you believes your version of events, you stop trusting your own reflection. These lines appear in HUNTED verbatim:

> I dried my face with a paper towel. Avoided the mirror. Opened the door. (HUNTED · Chapter 1)
