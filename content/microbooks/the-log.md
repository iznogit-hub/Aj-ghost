These pages were never meant to be read by the man they describe.

DAY 1

06:12. Subject awake. Sits up slowly. Holds right side of head for approx. 40 seconds.

06:20. Exits vehicle. Checks all four door handles before walking away. Checks driver's door twice.

06:31. Gas station restroom. Nine minutes.

06:44. Returns. Buys nothing.

07:02. Sitting in vehicle. Counting something. Lips moving. Fingers tapping the steering wheel.

Note: He counts things. Find out what.

DAY 2

05:58. Awake early. Dome light on at 02:15 and again at 03:40. Did not sleep well.

06:30. Walks the lot perimeter before restroom. Looks at every parked car. Does not look at this one.

09:10. Sits on curb in shade. Takes out phone. Looks at screen for four minutes. No call placed.

Note: There is a number he does not call. Find out whose.

13:00. Heat. Subject moves vehicle twelve feet to follow the shade of one tree. Gets out, checks handles, gets back in.

Note: Not lazy. Not careless. Tired in a way that looks like careless from a distance. People who walk past him see careless. That is useful.

DAY 3

06:05. Awake.

06:06. Stares at the glove compartment for a long time. Opens it. Takes out a piece of paper. Writes on it. Puts it back.

Note: He writes things down too.

Note: Find out what he writes.

11:20. Subject drops keys twice trying to unlock door. Hands not steady. Sits on the ground beside the car for six minutes with his eyes shut.

11:26. Stands. Tries again. Succeeds.

Note: He does not ask anyone for help. Three people walked past during those six minutes. None of them stopped.

Nobody stops. That is the whole point.

DAY 4

No entries before noon. Subject did not leave vehicle.

12:15. Exits. Walks toward the road. Stops at the edge of the lot. Turns around. Comes back.

Note: He wanted to go somewhere and could not decide where. Or he forgot where. The difference matters. Find out which.

16:45. Subject looks directly at this vehicle.

Eleven seconds.

Does not approach. Does not write it down. Goes back to his car and sits with both hands flat on the wheel.

Note: He notices more than he believes he notices. That is the thing to understand about him. The alarm in his head goes off all the time, so he has trained himself not to listen to it. He will see you and then talk himself out of having seen you.

Recommend more distance anyway.

DAY 5

Changed vehicles.

06:10. Subject awake. Same routine. Handles. Restroom. Counting.

07:30. Two young men argue near the ice machine. Loud. One shoves the other. Subject is out of his car before the second shove lands. Not running. Not shouting. Standing between them with his hands open at his sides.

They stop.

They leave.

Subject stands there after they go, breathing hard, looking at his own hands as if they belong to someone else.

Note: Still has the training. Still moves toward trouble when anyone else would move away. He thinks that part of him is gone. It isn't. It is just buried under everything else.

Note: That part is worth a great deal.

DAY 6

03:00. Light rain. Rare. Subject awake, watching it run down the windshield.

03:20. He has his forehead against the glass.

Note: Nothing to record.

Note: Recording it anyway.

DAY 7

06:12. Awake.

06:20. Handles. Driver's door twice.

06:31. Restroom. Nine minutes.

Note: Same as Day 1. To the minute, almost. A life folded down to the size of a parking space. He does not know how small it has gotten. Nobody has told him. Nobody is around to tell him.

Note: Someone will be.

Last entry for this week. The rule stands. Never the same car twice in one day. Never close enough to be sure of. Let him wonder.

Wondering is the part that wears a person down.
